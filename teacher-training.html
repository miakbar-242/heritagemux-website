Your Step-by-Step
Google Classroom Guide

Everything you need to start posting lessons, receiving work, and reviewing submissions — all from your phone or laptop.

Works on Phone & Laptop
Takes 15 Min to Learn
All 8 Classes
What You Will Do

Your Role as a Teacher

Every day you follow a simple 3-step routine — Record, Post, Review.

Step 1 — Record

Record a short lesson video on your phone. 5–10 min for Montessori, 10–15 min for Class 1–5.

Step 2 — Post

Open Google Classroom, create an Assignment, attach your video, set the due date, and post.

Step 3 — Review

Open submitted work, check it, leave a comment or mark, and click Return. The parent is notified automatically.

Post on a Schedule

Try to post at the same time every day. Morning is best — aim for before 9 AM.

First Time Only

Getting Set Up

The admin has already created your Google Classroom class and added you as the teacher.

1

Install the Google Classroom App

Do this once on your phone

On Android

Open Play Store → Search Google Classroom → Tap Install.

On iPhone

Open App Store → Search Google Classroom → Tap Get.

On Laptop

Open Chrome and go to classroom.google.com — no installation needed.

2

Sign In with Your School Gmail

Use the Gmail account given by the school

Open Google Classroom and tap Sign In

Use your school Gmail address and password.

Do NOT use your personal Gmail.

Your class will already be there

Tap it to open. If you do not see it, contact the Admin immediately.

Turn on Notifications

Settings → Apps → Google Classroom → Notifications → Turn ON.

3

The 3 Tabs Inside Your Class

Takes 2 minutes to learn

Stream Tab

The class notice board. Parents and students can see this.

Classwork Tab

Most important. This is where you create and manage Assignments.

People Tab

Shows all students and guardians. Admin manages this.

Step 1 — Every Day

Recording Your Lesson Video

Two options — choose whichever is easier for you.

Option A — Phone Camera Recommended

  • Switch to Video mode
  • Prop phone against something stable
  • Stand near a whiteboard
  • Speak clearly and slowly
  • Tap Record and teach
  • Tap Stop — video saves to Gallery

Option B — Loom Laptop Users

  • Go to loom.com and sign up free
  • Install the Loom app on your laptop
  • Click New Recording
  • Record your lesson
  • Loom gives you a link — copy it
Video Tips

Do's and Don'ts

    Do This

  • Keep videos short — 5–10 min Montessori, 10–15 min Class 1–5
  • Record in a quiet room with good lighting
  • Write the date and topic on the board first
  • Start with "Assalamu Alaikum, today we are learning..."
  • End by clearly stating the task

    Avoid This

  • Videos longer than 15 minutes
  • Noisy or dark recording spaces
  • Pointing the camera at a window
  • Forgetting to state the homework task at the end
Step 2 — Every Day

Posting a Lesson

Follow these steps every time you post a new lesson.

A

Upload Video to Google Drive

Do this before opening Classroom

Open Google Drive with your school Gmail

Open your class folder

Admin has created a folder (e.g. Class 3 Videos). Always save there.

Upload your video

Tap + → Upload → select from Gallery. Use WiFi — videos are large.

Copy the video link

Tap ⋮ → Share → Copy Link. Loom users: just copy your Loom link.

B

Create the Assignment

How students receive the lesson

Classwork → + Create → Assignment

Write the Title

Format: Subject — Topic — Date. Example: Maths — Addition — 25 March

Write Instructions

Short and clear. Example: "Watch the video below. Complete Exercise 3, page 18 in your notebook."

Attach the Video Link

Tap Add → Link → paste → Add Link.

Set Due Date

Next day for Classes 1–5. 48 hours for Montessori.

Tap Assign

Parents get an email notification automatically. They are notified the moment you tap Assign.

Step 3 — Every Day

Reviewing Submitted Work

Review and return work within 24 hours of submission.

1

Open the Submitted Work

See what each student submitted

Classwork → Assignment → Tap it

Summary shows: Turned In, Assigned, and Missing.

Tap a student's name to view their work

2

Leave a Comment & Grade

Private feedback for student and parent

Tap "Private Comment"

✅ "Excellent! Question 4 correct. Check Q7 again."
✅ "Well done! Beautiful handwriting today." For Montessori, add a note for the parent too.

Add a Grade (Classes 3–5 only)

Type marks e.g. 8/10. Classes 1–2 and Montessori: use comments only.

3

Return the Work

Notifies the parent you have reviewed it

Tap Return

Parent and student notified automatically. Always Return — even perfect work needs returning.

Follow up on missing submissions

Leave a comment: "Please submit your work." or inform the admin.
