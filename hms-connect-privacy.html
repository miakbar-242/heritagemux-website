Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

HMS Connect is the official communication app for Heritage Montessori School. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Parent name and phone number
  • Child name and class
  • Google account email (for teachers and admin only)
  • Messages sent through the app
  • Staff attendance records

How We Use Information

  • To enable communication between school and parents
  • To manage teacher and parent accounts
  • To track staff attendance
  • To send school announcements and updates

Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase cloud services hosted by Google LLC.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with any third parties outside of Heritage Montessori School.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account by contacting the school admin.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for parents and school staff only. Children do not use this app directly.

Contact

For privacy questions contact: info@heritagemux.com
